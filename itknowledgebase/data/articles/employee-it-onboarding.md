# Employee IT Onboarding Guide

## Purpose

This guide describes the standard technology setup for a new Northstar Labs employee. The hiring manager, People Operations, IT Provisioning, and the new employee each own part of the process. Requests submitted late may delay access on the employee's first day.

## Before the start date

People Operations creates the worker record at least ten business days before the start date. The hiring manager then completes the IT onboarding form, confirming the employee's legal name, preferred display name, start date, office or shipping location, department, manager, and job role.

The standard equipment package contains a managed laptop, power adapter, headset, and one external monitor for office-based employees. Non-standard equipment requires a business justification and cost-center owner approval. IT Provisioning ships remote equipment only to the address recorded in the onboarding request and sends tracking details to the hiring manager.

IT creates the primary company identity no earlier than five business days before the start date. The account remains blocked until the agreed activation window. Application access beyond the standard role package must be requested separately; onboarding does not automatically grant production, finance, human-resources, or administrator access.

## First-day setup

The employee receives an activation message through the personal contact method held by People Operations. During activation, the employee creates a compliant password, registers at least two MFA methods when possible, and accepts the acceptable-use notice. Credentials must not be sent to the manager or included in a calendar invitation.

After sign-in, the employee should verify:

1. Disk encryption and endpoint protection report a healthy status.
2. Email, calendar, chat, and the support portal are accessible.
3. The correct department groups and shared workspaces appear.
4. The recovery phone or hardware key belongs to the employee.
5. Operating-system updates complete successfully.

The employee should restart the laptop after the initial management setup, even if no restart prompt appears.

## Standard software

The managed software catalog provides approved browsers, productivity tools, the VPN client, conferencing software, and role-specific development tools. Employees should use the catalog rather than downloading installers from the internet. Software not listed in the catalog requires a software request and may require Security or Procurement review.

Local administrator rights are not included in the standard package. Engineering employees who need elevated actions should request the approved temporary-elevation service. Permanent local administrator access is granted only by exception.

## Manager responsibilities

The manager confirms on the first day that the employee received the correct device and can reach the resources needed for initial work. The manager must request missing business application access through the application access process and should not ask another employee to share credentials or data as a workaround.

Within the first week, the manager reviews the employee's group memberships and removes access that is unnecessary for the role. Problems with delivery, identity activation, or device health should be submitted to the Service Desk under **People Changes > New Starter**.
